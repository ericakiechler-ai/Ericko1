# 889-GEN — calculation review and QC record

**Tool:** `src/889-gen.html` — GE Multilin 889 generator protection × Omicron CMC 356
**Scope:** every `compute()`, the impedance-plane injection model, the differential
split, the channel mapper, the limit checks, the vector calculator, the presets and the
sample configuration. Each injected quantity was traced from setting to channel with the
sample ratios (5000:5 both ends, 200:5 ground CT, 13800:120 VT, 7970:240 grounding
transformer, 100 MVA at 13.8 kV → 4183.7 A primary, 4.184 A secondary, Zbase 16.56 Ω
secondary), and the notes were checked against what the code actually does.
**Outcome:** released. Two medium findings and four low ones fixed, on top of the REF
scaling fix made earlier; two items left open for the manual.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | **50G / 51G used the terminal CT ratio for the dedicated ground CT input.** Pickup conversions went through `ocPickup` with the 5000:5 terminal ratio, so the default 0.5 ×CT read as 2500 A primary instead of 100 A on the 200:5 ground CT, and any pickup entered in primary amps injected 25× too little. The element also offered a terminal/neutral CT-set choice the ground input does not have. | `gPickup()` converts through the ground CT ratio for every unit; the CT-set field is dropped for ground elements; the readout names the ground CT; the channel note says *into the ground CT input*. |
| 2 | Medium | **21 phase-to-phase loop carried 3V0 = full L-N voltage.** Vb = Vm∠0, Vc = Vm∠180 with Va = N∠90 sums to N. The loop (Vb−Vc)/(Ib−Ic) was right, but every 21 test placed a 69 V zero-sequence voltage and a large V2 on the phase VTs. | Same construction as the 850 fix: Vb = −Va/2 + Vm·û, Vc = −Va/2 − Vm·û with û the prefault Vbc direction. Loop unchanged, V0 = 0, rotation honoured. Sheet reports the actual L2 phasor. |
| 3 | Low | **32 directional power over-injected by 1/cos(MTA).** I = P/(3V·cos θ) placed at −θ makes the operating quantity P·cosθ + Q·sinθ equal P/cosθ. No error at θ = 0 (the default). | I = P/(3V) at −θ (180° − θ for reverse). Readout and note follow. |
| 4 | Low | **87G characteristic stepped at Break 1.** Below Break 1 the required differential was the pickup alone, then jumped to slope 1 × restraint above it — a 0.10 → 0.20 pu step on the sample. Slope 1 applies below Break 1 with the pickup as a floor. | `req()` returns max(pickup, slope1·Ir) up to Break 2. Unchanged at the sample restraint points 0.5 and 1.0 pu; different at 1.0–2.0 pu. The 845's 87T carries the same construction — see open item 2 and the follow-up commit. |
| 5 | Low | **Phase rotation hardcoded** outside `P3()`: the 46 reverse-rotation sets, the 47 sets, the 67N polarising set, the 27/59 single-phase sets, the 50/27 current set, all eight vector presets and `seq()`. | `rotS()`/`rotA()`/`P3rev()` helpers everywhere; `seq()` exchanges p and n under ACB. |
| 6 | Low | Duplicate `inputmode`; no Setup-first on a fresh load. | Fixed. |

## Open — needs the 889 manual

1. **IAC Extremely Inverse, constant E** — as in the 845, 850 and 869: the tool carries
   −0.2461 where GE publishes +0.2461. Not changed offline.
2. **87G slope 2 and the transition.** Above Break 2 the tool uses a line from the slope‑1
   value at Break 2 with slope 2 (continuous), and a straight slope‑1 line between the
   breaks. The UR family instead runs slope 2 through the origin above Break 2 with a
   cubic spline between the breaks; at the sample's 7 pu restraint point that is 3.5 pu of
   differential against the tool's 1.1 pu. Which the 889 (and the 845) uses must be read
   from the manual. The restraint definition (max, average, sum) is already a setting with
   a *confirm in manual* hint; the slope‑2 form should be confirmed with it. The tool's
   note to prove the first differential point against the relay's own metering covers the
   customer in the meantime.
3. **Vector-group and CT-set conventions on `iTriples`** were not verified against the
   889's terminal-end / neutral-end input naming.

## Accepted simplifications (documented, not changed)

- The 21 loop and 40/78 points use a fixed held current; the Setup checks flag when the
  deepest zone would exceed the voltage limit.
- The 50/27 test current set sits at −75° (a nominal fault angle), not at the machine's
  load angle.
- The core‑balance/ground CT secondary is assumed to be the value entered, with no
  burden or accuracy‑class correction.
