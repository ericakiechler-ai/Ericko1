It is a calculator, not a relay. Every value shown is computed from settings typed in by its user. The tool does not read the device, does not know its firmware revision or its as-built wiring, and cannot tell whether the settings entered match the ones in service. Treat what it produces as a proposal to be checked against the relay and the drawings.

Verify before you inject. Confirm the CT and VT ratios, the curve constants and at least the first timing point against the device's own prediction before accepting the rest of a table. Curve constants move between firmware revisions; if the first point disagrees, every other time in that table is wrong by the same factor.
