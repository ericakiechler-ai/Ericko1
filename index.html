Every tool works the same way. Enter the settings as they are in the device; read back what to put on each channel of the test set, and what the relay should do about it. This is one step of one function on the 7SJ85, at its sample feeder.

The suite

Six devices, one way of working

All six share a core, a set of conventions and a layout, so moving between them costs nothing. Each one carries the functions that device actually has, and the engineering that device actually needs.

What ships

The calculator is the middle of the job, not the end of it

Each device comes with the plan built from its own tool, so the values on the test record cannot drift from the values that were calculated.

The tools

One HTML file each. No install, no network, no dependencies — they open on a locked-down field laptop and work with the Wi-Fi off. Keep the seven files in one folder and the links above open them. 6 files · 137 functions

Test plans

Generated from each tool, not typed beside it: a step form to work through and a channel long form for pasting into a Test Universe module table. 638 steps · 4356 channel rows

Excel workbooks

The plan as a test record — the values laid out, two shaded columns to fill in, and counters that total the results as they are entered. 6 workbooks · 4 sheets each

Build guides

Verification

Every number was checked against something else

A calculator that is confidently wrong is worse than no calculator. Each tool was run headlessly and its output recomputed independently before it shipped. A sample of what that caught and confirmed:

845 · Dyn1
Through current nets to Idiff 0.0000 on all three phases. Building the W2 triple at 180° instead of 150° leaves 0.518 pu standing — which is 2 sin 15°, not a wiring fault.
850 · k0
A ground loop driven at 73.79° reads back 68°, the pure line angle, confirming the compensation is applied once and not twice. Five fault positions report exactly where they were placed.
869 · thermal
Fit mode reproduces its own fit point exactly — 10.000 s at 6 × FLA. The sample motor fails its hot start at 7.49 s against 8.00 s to accelerate, deliberately.
889 · impedance
Round-trips exactly through symmetrical-component decomposition; through current nets to zero differential.
7SJ85 · 67Ns
The operate sector runs to ±75.5° from the axis, not ±90°: it closes where the watt-metric component falls to threshold. All ten verdicts agree with an independent recomputation.
7SD82 · 87L
Thirty slope points across all three restraint definitions land on the intended (Irest, Idiff), recomputed from the channel table. Through current gives Idiff 0.0000.

They are calculators, not relays. Every value is computed from settings typed in by the user. The tools do not read the device, do not know its firmware revision or as-built wiring, and cannot tell whether the settings entered match the ones in service. What they produce is a proposal to be checked against the relay and the drawings before any current or voltage reaches plant.
